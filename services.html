What we do

Taxation, Audit & Consulting Services in Harare

Fully integrated tax, audit and consulting services — tailored to give you an effective route toward your business ambitions. We work with owner-managed businesses, farming operations, hospitality groups and NGOs who need a firm that understands both the compliance side and the commercial reality of running a business in Zimbabwe.

01 / TAX

Taxation

Income Tax · PAYE · VAT · ZIMRA registration

Best suited to businesses that want tax handled proactively, not just at year-end — from first ZIMRA registration through to ongoing return filing.

  • Online Income Tax, PAYE and VAT returns
  • Tax registration with ZIMRA
  • Tax clearance certificate applications
  • Advice on the latest tax matters and trends
  • Personal and business-level tax strategy
  • Ongoing tax consulting on a continuous basis
  • Representation during ZIMRA queries and audits
  • Withholding tax and capital gains tax computations
02 / AUDIT

Audit

Audit & assurance · internal audit · forensic audit

Best suited to companies needing statutory assurance, lenders or shareholders requiring independent verification, or boards wanting a genuine read on internal controls.

  • Audit & assurance engagements
  • Internal audit and controls review
  • Forensic audit and investigation
  • IFRS-compliant reporting
  • Systems of internal control design
  • Peer-reviewed, quality-controlled delivery
  • Stock counts and asset verification
  • Special-purpose and agreed-upon-procedures reports
03 / CONSULT

Consulting

Bookkeeping · advisory · company secretarial

Best suited to owner-managed businesses that need day-to-day financial systems built and maintained, plus a Director-level sounding board for bigger decisions.

  • Bookkeeping & accounting
  • Forecasting and budget preparation
  • Management and year-end accounts
  • Payroll management
  • Company secretarial services
  • Financial due diligence & valuations
  • Business proposals, mergers & acquisitions
  • Investment analysis
  • New company registration and CIPZ compliance
How we work

From first call to ongoing delivery

A consistent, four-step process regardless of which service you engage us for.

01

Discovery call

A short conversation to understand your business, current systems and what's prompting the enquiry.

02

Scoping & proposal

We confirm the scope of work, timeline and fees in writing before anything begins.

03

Onboarding & setup

Accounts, registrations and systems are set up or reviewed, with a clear handover of what we need from you.

04

Delivery & review

Ongoing filing, reporting and advisory, with a periodic review to check the engagement still fits your business.
